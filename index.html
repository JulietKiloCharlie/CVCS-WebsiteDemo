02 / Charcoal Drain Filters

The only one
in Ireland.

We are Ireland's only fleet washing company that provides, fits and maintains charcoal drain filters, tested to comply with all relevant EU regulations.

Every wash produces runoff full of oils, detergents and road contaminants. Our charcoal filtration captures them before they reach the drain, protecting the environment and keeping your business compliant.

01

Provide

EU-compliant charcoal filtration units, specified for your site.

02

Fit

03

Maintain
